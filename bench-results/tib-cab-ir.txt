nc.cab.ir on tib: the core's nam::Linear against AudioDSPTools' PartitionedConvolution
=======================================================================================

2026-09-25. tib (Tinker Board S, Cortex-A17), kernel 7.2.6-tib1 PREEMPT_RT, 1416 MHz cap, core 3
isolated, namchain-soak --graph <rig> --seconds 20 --cpu 3 (SCHED_FIFO 80), wall-clock pacing, armv7
NEON build (-mfpu=neon-vfpv4). IR: York Audio ORNG 412 V30 421v-CN, 48 kHz, 24000 taps, truncated
by hand to the shorter lengths.

"before" is namchain 412e151: nam::Linear from the vendored core (2563c0f), Auto implementation.
"after" is PartitionedConvolution from rikkus/AudioDSPTools partitioned-ir b505422, which also caps
the IR at 8192 taps (the before build convolved all 24000).

  2048     20.9 (2)       4.3  (0)       36.6 (2)        9.5  (1)
  8192     62.5 (0)       10.0 (0)       117.0 (462)     16.2 (2)
  24000    111.2 (229)    9.0  (0)       118.3 (709)     19.3 (0)

The after column matches NAMBench's own tib figures for b505422 at 8192 taps (9.89% at 64, 16.0%
at 32), measured in a different harness.

0 render-path allocations. The remaining overruns are not the cab's: A2-Full alone in the same
