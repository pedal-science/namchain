A2-Full and A2-Full + full-length cab on piv
============================================

2026-09-26. piv (Raspberry Pi 500, Cortex-A76 at 2.4 GHz, no cap), kernel
7.0.0-1017-raspi-realtime PREEMPT_RT, cores 2-3 isolated, services confined to 0-1
(pedal.project devices/piv at 8740fd2), namchaind stopped. aarch64 Release build
(-march=armv8-a -mtune=cortex-a76, A2 planar NEON on). `sudo pedal-rt namchain-soak --graph <rig>
--cpu 3` (SCHED_FIFO 80), wall-clock pacing, tests/input.wav, 30 s cool-down between runs,
35-36 C, throttled=0x0 after. IR: York Audio ORNG 412 V30 421v-CNT, 48 kHz, 24000 taps, which
nc.cab.ir truncates to 8192.

p99 as % of the deadline, max block, overruns:

                        q64 (1.333 ms)              q32 (0.667 ms)
  A2-Full alone
    79939b4 (30 s)      7.23%  max 0.35 ms  0       7.96%  max 0.29 ms  0
    04a1fc8 (60 s)      7.33%  max 1.22 ms  0       8.03%  max 0.49 ms  0
  A2-Full + cab
    79939b4 (30 s)      8.68%  max 1.04 ms  0      10.72%  max 1.13 ms  2
    04a1fc8 (60 s)     15.39%  max 0.99 ms  0      24.09%  max 1.16 ms  5

79939b4 convolves with rikkus/AudioDSPTools' PartitionedConvolution (partition multiplies spread
across the quiet callbacks); 04a1fc8 with the core's nam::Linear, since the fork is deprecated.
nam::Linear's burst roughly doubles the cab's share of the p99, and is still far inside the
deadline at q64.

The worst blocks are ~1 ms whichever convolver runs, and A2-Full alone had one of 1.22 ms. At q64
that leaves little margin at the very tail; at q32 it overruns. The source of those single slow
blocks is not identified. Plan 1.8's hour-long soak on the device is the measurement that
decides whether q64 holds.
