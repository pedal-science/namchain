E2 item 1: Lite against Full, every model in the shipping library
==========================================================================

2026-10-06. Tool: namchain 083de32, `namchain-warm-bench slim`, built for aarch64 in the image
build's own container (-march=armv8-a -mtune=cortex-a76, what scripts/deploy-engine --host vip
produces) and run under colima on the Mac. Cross-checked against the Mac's namchain-render on
three models: identical to the last digit, so the host does not change these numbers.

Input: pedal.project guitar-di.wav, 48 kHz mono 24-bit, 56.66 s, peak -1.32 dBFS, rms -23.64
dBFS, crest 22.3 dB, md5 f9ab7f8f7ece122917d9701c3360a32f, at its recorded level. That level
was checked not to matter: trimming the input 22.7 dB moved the relative null by 1.4 dB.
Models: the 52 .nam files under /var/lib/pedal/library/models -- all SlimmableContainer, all
48 kHz, each a 3-channel submodel at max_value 0.5 (Lite) and an 8-channel one at 1.0 (Full).
No cab here: 35 of the 52 carry their own, and putting the same rigs through York Audio's
ORNG 412 V30 421v-CNT moves every relative null by 1-2 dB and changes no conclusion.

Method. Render each model twice over the whole input, --slim 0.0 and --slim 1.0, and compare
sample by sample: both submodels are zero-latency (the best integer lag is 0, and no worse)
than 1 sample for any model), so this is a null and not an alignment artefact. At each of 11
switch points 5 s apart, judge a 5 ms equal-power crossfade from one submodel to the other
with namchain::glitch -- continuity, dropout, high frequency and convergence -- in both
directions, against whichever submodel ran after the switch.

with, and it is the law that thumps. Every row above carries a fade excess of +2.6 to +3.0 dB --
the fade adds energy because the two submodels are correlated (rho 0.91 to 1.00) -- and the owner
heard it as "a quiet, short thud at each switch from full to lite". The glitch detector cannot see
a level bump, which is why the handover column says clean. With the fade's power compensated for
the correlation it measured, all 104 rows come out at 0.00 dB excess and the two marginal findings
also clear: e2-fade-thump.txt has the measurement, the fix and what it means for rt::Crossfade.
