E2 item 1: Lite against Full, every model in the shipping library
==========================================================================

2026-10-06. Tool: namchain 083de32, `namchain-warm-bench slim`, built for aarch64 in the image
build's own container (-march=armv8-a -mtune=cortex-a76, what scripts/deploy-engine --host vip
produces) and run under colima on the Mac. Cross-checked against the Mac's namchain-render on
three models: identical to the last digit, so the host does not change these numbers.

Input: pedal.project guitar-di.wav, 48 kHz mono 24-bit, 56.66 s, peak -1.32 dBFS, rms -23.64
dBFS, crest 22.3 dB, md5 f9ab7f8f7ece122917d9701c3360a32f, at its recorded level. That level
was checked not to matter: trimming the input 22.7 dB moved the relative null by 1.4 dB.
Models: the 52 .nam files under /var/lib/pedal/library/models -- all SlimmableContainer, all
48 kHz, each a 3-channel submodel at max_value 0.5 (Lite) and an 8-channel one at 1.0 (Full).
No cab here: 35 of the 52 carry their own, and putting the same rigs through York Audio's
ORNG 412 V30 421v-CNT moves every relative null by 1-2 dB and changes no conclusion.

Method. Render each model twice over the whole input, --slim 0.0 and --slim 1.0, and compare
sample by sample: both submodels are zero-latency (the best integer lag is 0, and no worse)
than 1 sample for any model), so this is a null and not an alignment artefact. At each of 11
switch points 5 s apart, judge a 5 ms equal-power crossfade from one submodel to the other
with namchain::glitch -- continuity, dropout, high frequency and convergence -- in both
directions, against whichever submodel ran after the switch.

  null         |Lite - Full| over the whole input, in dBFS
  wrong-model  the worst |Lite - Full| in the 200 ms after a switch: what a listener hears
               while a Lite stands in for the Full, which is tier 2's first step
  handover     switch points on which the crossfade passed every rule

Reading
-------
Lite and Full are not one network at two widths. They are two separately trained
submodels of the same rig: in the worst model here, 56 of the Lite's 1871 weights appear in
the Full's (3%), and the vendored reference A2.nam shares 4 of 1871 (0.2%). namchain's own
daemon doc already says as much about the budget path (docs/daemon.md, model_slimmed: "a
Lite does not sound like the Full the rig names").

So tier 2's premise -- "Lite and Full are separate submodels ... the handover is a small
change of sound" -- is false as written, on every model in the library. The two are
level-matched (median 0.09 dB) and time-aligned, so what is left is timbre, and it is not
small: the null is 9 to 40 dB below the signal (median 21.7), which is 20 to 50 dB above the
-60 dBFS this project treats as its audibility bar. It is worst exactly where it matters,
on the amp and full-rig models, and best on the nine SD-1 pedal captures (-35 to -40 dB).

The crossfade itself is not the problem: 102 of 104 rows are clean at every switch point,
and the two that are not are marginal continuity findings 20-40% over the second-difference
bar, at 55 s where the DI is a decaying tail (oracle peak -36 dBFS). Where the detector did
flag something at a musical level -- an HF excess of 3.4-3.9 dB on two SVT models -- the
absolute level of the excess is -95 dBFS, i.e. 73 dB below the signal.

What a listener hears in tier 2 is therefore not a click. It is the wrong-sounding rig for
the tens of ms the Full takes to catch up, then a clean crossfade to the right one. Whether
that is acceptable is the listening test, and it is the only part of item 1 left:
pedal.project build/e2/audition/ has Lite, Full and an alternating handover for the worst,
the median, the best and the two flagged models.
