The splice bridge on the device: what was measured, and what could not be
=========================================================================

2026-09-15 evening, `1cfa27d`+working tree. Evidence: soak-20min-paired.*, soak-8min-paired.*,
soak-20min-paired-sparse.*, soak-ab2-{a1,b1,b2}.*, soak-5min-bridge.* (afternoon),
soak-1h-ring6-cap4.* and the A/B in queue-floor.txt.

The question this was meant to answer
-------------------------------------
The bridge is verified at the unit level: `tests/test_splice_fader.cpp` runs plan §10.3's own detector
over a raw splice and a repaired one and reports the numbers by which the shape was chosen (a hold
scores 0.052, a decaying extrapolation 0.0521, the Hermite bridge 0.0097 against a limit of 0.0136).
What that leaves open is whether the *loop* repairs the *device's* splices -- so `--splice-verify` was
built to judge each splice on the emitted audio, with the raw splice as its oracle, in the same run.

It is a paired design: for one splice the verifier builds two spans over the same real emitted history,
the second differing only in the period the bridge repaired. Both are analysed over the same window,
so "raw 0.33 -> emitted 0.01" would be one measurement of one splice rather than a comparison of two
runs whose splices are never the same.

What it found instead
---------------------
Three regimes were measured, and none of them produced the measurement it was built for:

  * **A sane configuration produces no splices at all, in this session.** Cap 3 and cap 4 with a
    6-period ring, 20 and 15 minutes each, no floor: **0 drops, 0 xruns, 0 bridges**. The same cap-4
    configuration produced 13-18 drops per 900 s this afternoon, so the drop rate is strongly
    session-dependent and this session was a clean one. There was nothing to judge.
  * **Dense splices cannot be judged, and the verifier says so rather than guessing.** With drops
    forced every four blocks (cap 1, 858 splices in 60 s) the "reference" region is made of other
    clicks: the reference rose to 0.0918 and the worst to 0.3362. The numbers describe the
    configuration, not the repair. The verifier now judges a splice only when the preceding five
    periods are splice-free, and reports how many it judged.
  * **A thrashing configuration is not repaired by a bridge, and that is a real finding.** Cap 2 with
    the floor at the same level is the squeezed-queue configuration the earlier work warned about
    (bounds one period apart), and it armed the bridge **236 841 times in 480 s -- once every two
    blocks**. 457 splices had a clean enough neighbourhood to judge, and they came out
    **raw 0.3277 -> emitted 0.3717**: the emitted signal was *worse*. The reason is structural rather
    than a bug: the bridge assumes clean audio either side of one splice, and when splices arrive
    every other block the repair overlaps itself into a chain of bridges, each one bridging the
    previous one's output. The repair has a duty-cycle limit, and above roughly 5 % of blocks it is
    not repairing anything.

The last of those is now reported by the tool: a run whose bridge fires on more than 5 % of blocks
prints a warning saying that the numbers below describe a chain of bridges rather than a splice, so
the failure cannot be read as a measurement.

Where that leaves the claim
---------------------------
Verified:
  * the shape, by the detector, with the numbers that chose it (unit level);
  * the wiring, on the device -- the bridge arms on real dropped and inserted periods, and the oracle
    stays exact (`max|diff| = 0` over 240 000 frames) in the same runs, with 0 render-path allocations;
  * the passthrough, by construction: an unarmed bridge copies, so a run that enables it and has no
    splices is bit-identical to one that never did.
Not verified on device:
  * the repaired splice's continuity *on a real isolated splice*, because no sane configuration in
    this session produced one. The instrument for it exists, is paired, and is exercised -- it judged
    955 and then 457 splices -- but only in regimes where the answer is "this configuration is not
    the one to judge".

The run to take next, and the reason it is a run rather than a change: cap 4 with `--splice-verify` on,
for an hour, **in a session that behaves like this afternoon's** (13-18 drops per 900 s). Then the
verifier has a dozen isolated splices with clean neighbourhoods, and the paired raw-versus-emitted
numbers are the evidence the bridge deserves. Nothing in the code needs to change to take it.

Also in this session: the balanced A/B of "cap 4 alone" against "cap 4 + floor 2 + a 1.33 ms bridge"
was started as A B B A and **stopped after three arms, all three with 0 drops, 0 insertions, 0 xruns
and 0 bridges**. With every arm an empty event set there was nothing for a fourth to add, and the
honest reading of the three is not "the arms are equal" but "this session could not distinguish
them". Its files are kept as soak-ab2-{a1,b1,b2}.*, and the a2 arm was deliberately not run.
